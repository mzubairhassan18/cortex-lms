How payment works

Card payments are not switched on yet. Until they are, paid plans are activated by bank transfer — the amount moves directly, Cortex never sees your card, and there is no recurring charge you have to remember to cancel.

Paying for Pro or Team

  1. Pick a plan above and sign in.
  2. Transfer the amount to the bank details shown on the payment screen, using your Cortex user ID as the reference.
  3. Click I have paid and paste the transfer reference.
  4. An administrator matches it against the account and activates your plan — normally the same day.

Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Nothing auto-renews.

What counts as a request?

One message you send to the model. Reading, searching, exporting, graphing and everything offline costs nothing and never counts against your daily limit.

Why is my own model key required?

Cortex stores your provider key in your own profile, encrypted at rest, and proxies the call so your browser never has to talk to the provider directly. You pay that provider for tokens; we do not mark them up.

What happens if I hit my limit?

The counter resets at midnight UTC. Nothing is deleted — your conversations, notes and summaries stay exactly where they are, and everything offline keeps working while you wait.

Can I downgrade?

Yes. Downgrading takes effect at the end of the paid period; the account falls back to the Free limits and nothing is removed. If you are over the Free limits, the oldest conversations stay saved but stop loading until you free up room or upgrade again.
